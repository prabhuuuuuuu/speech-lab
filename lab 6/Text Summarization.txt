A university receives a collection of lengthy student-feedback reports containing information about teaching quality, infrastructure, laboratory facilities, faculty interaction and student satisfaction. You are given a document containing multiple paragraphs of student feedback. Develop an NLP-based summarization system that produces a concise summary of the document. Your system must:

a) Preprocess the input document by performing sentence segmentation, tokenization and removal of unnecessary text.

b) Implement all graph/statistical-based approach to identify the most important sentences and generate an extractive summary.

1. Frequency-based summarization

2. TF-IDF-based summarization

3. TextRank

4. LexRank

5. Feature-based sentence scoring

 

c) Implement all deep-learning-based approach to generate an extractive and abstractive summary.

1. RNN-Based Summarization

2. LSTM for Text Summarization

3. Encoder–Decoder / Seq2Seq Model

4. Self Attention Mechanism

5. Transformer-Based Summarization

6. BERT for Extractive Summarization

7. BART

8. PEGASUS

9. T5

d) Compare summaries of different techniques with a reference summary using ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2 and ROUGE-L.
