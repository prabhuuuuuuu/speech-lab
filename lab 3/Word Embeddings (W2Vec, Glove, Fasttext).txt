A news classification system needs to represent words as numerical vectors. The training corpus contains both common words and several rare/unseen words such as:  "The students are learning artificial intelligence and machinelearning techniques. The students use machine-learning algorithms for intelligent applications." Your task is to investigate how Word2Vec, GloVe, and FastText represent words and determine which embedding technique is most suitable for this corpus.

 

Part A – Corpus Preparation

Create a corpus containing at least 20–30 sentences related to one domain such as:

l Artificial Intelligence

l Healthcare

l Cybersecurity

l Agriculture

l E-commerce

Perform:

l Tokenization

l Lowercasing

l Removal of unnecessary punctuation

l Optional stop-word removal

Preserve some morphologically related words such as: learn, learning, learned, learner and are/compound words such as: machine-learning, cybersecurity, healthcare

 

Part B – Word2Vec

Implement Word2Vec using both CBOW and Skip-gram. Experiment with at least two different window sizes, for example: window = 2 and window = 4

For each model:

l Train the embeddings.

l Display the vector representation of selected words.

l Find the 5 most similar words for: learning, machine, intelligence, student.

l Compare CBOW and Skip-gram results.

 

Part C – GloVe

Implement or use a GloVe implementation to obtain word vectors from the same corpus. Analyze:

l Similar words obtained for the selected words.

l Whether words occurring in similar global contexts are represented closely.

l Differences between GloVe and Word2Vec similarity results.

 

Part D – FastText

Train a FastText model on the same corpus. Test the following groups: learn, learning, learned, learner

Then test a word that does not occur in the training corpus, for example: machine-learning

Determine whether FastText can generate an embedding for the unseen word.
