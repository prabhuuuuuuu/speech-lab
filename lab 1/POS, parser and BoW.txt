Take an english text and speech as a input sample and perform the following

 A) POS Tagging:
Tokenize the sentence and audio.  Assign an appropriate Part-of-Speech (POS) tag to each word. Identify the nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, conjunctions, and other grammatical categories.

B) Parsing:
Construct and visualize a constituency parse tree  and dependency parse tree for the sentence and the audio file. Analyze how the two clauses are related.

C) Bag of Words:
Create a Bag of Words representation for the sentence/audio. Display:

· The vocabulary

· The corresponding word-frequency vector

D) Analysis and Interpretation:
Based on the POS tags, parse structure, and BoW representation, discuss:

· Which information is preserved by BoW?

· Which linguistic information is lost by BoW?

· Why can parsing provide information that BoW cannot?

E) Higher-Order Challenge:
Modify the input text sentence by changing the word order
